Working toward measuring without a card

Card-free capture is the goal. The card gives this browser photo test a known scale while we develop and test the sweep route. The companion app source uses ARKit and automatic rectangle detection without a reference card. It must first be built and installed using Xcode on a Mac. Safari cannot supply those ARKit measurements. The photo-reference route works here now; both routes remain unverified until compared with real boards.
