## Enter and play
1. BUILD & GOVERN → normal Admiral verification → OPEN STORMWAKE. FUN is a separate Admiral Program, never a business vessel.
2. On the title screen choose Enter the harbor. Move with the left thumbstick; drag the world to look around. Jump, Camera recenter and contextual Interact are on the right. Walk to Bootstrap and start The Broken Passage.
3. Explore the quay, collect three repair parts, jump the small gold-edged break in the pier, balance the mechanism, board the launch and bring the chartmaker to the lighthouse. There is no required countdown. Missed jumps return to the last safe dock; collected repairs remain.

At the helm: push forward/back for throttle/reverse and left/right to steer. Leave the loading berth straight before turning. Release the stick or hold Brake near a marked landing; Dock appears when close and slow. Reverse away from a blocked bow. Boat exit is allowed only at marked safe berths.

Keyboard alternative: WASD/arrows; Space to jump or brake; E to interact; drag to look; Escape to pause. No gamepad or purchase is required.
