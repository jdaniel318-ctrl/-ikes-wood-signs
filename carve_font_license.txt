Fleetcraft additions — six outlined public reference fonts

Official Google Fonts source snapshot: e7db6ca603fd4862010383ff17ffd6cc4ffac1c0.
Generated by tools/build_fleetcraft_assets.py with fontTools 4.61.1.
Cinzel is instantiated at wght=700 before extracting outlines and metrics.
No code point is silently substituted. Kerning is retained where supplied by
the original font: Rye has no kerning table. Lobster uses its mapped base glyphs
and pair kerning; contextual alternates and ligature shaping are not applied.
Twelve invisible two-point closed marker contours in Rye are omitted because
they have no filled area. Visible glyph curves and advance widths are retained.
The added fonts use clockwise outer contours in font coordinates. Mirrored
source components are reversed, with their counters, without changing their
curves or nonzero filled appearance, so overlapping fonts and emblems union
consistently in CAM. The original two font objects remain exactly unchanged.
The sixteen emblems are original filled Fleetcraft vector artwork authored in
the generator, with nonzero winding, exact bounds and SHA-256 of each SVG path.
