# Ike's True Grain 7.1.0

True Grain is the refinement pass after the successful Plankwright customer voyage. It keeps the seven-step structure and improves clarity without claiming unproven AI capability.

## Changes
- internal ordering header is more compact;
- landing journey now matches the actual photo-first flow;
- recognition surfaces measurement readiness, safe-margin preview protection, and the fact that obstacle avoidance remains a Sea Trial item;
- live lettering and stored approved preview use a conservative inboard width until usable-area segmentation is proven;
- lettering choices are larger;
- required contact fields are visibly marked;
- final review compresses further on shorter landscape viewports.
