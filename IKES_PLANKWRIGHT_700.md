# Ike's Plankwright 7.0.0

## Mission
Turn Ike's customer journey from a ten-step form into a plank-first visual design experience while preserving the exact-plank photo, explicit approval, project isolation, test safety, and final production record.

## Candidate flow
1. Welcome / start with the plank.
2. Photograph the exact plank.
3. Plank Recognition scaffold: detect photo geometry, infer orientation, and explicitly disclose that real-world measurement still requires calibration. Posted price remains a temporary confirmation fallback.
4. Design Your Sign workspace: wording, lettering style, optional lettering-reference photo, finish/color, top marker, orientation, and live exact-plank preview in one surface.
5. Explicit design approval checkpoint.
6. Contact information after design approval.
7. Compact final review and required production approval.
8. Confirmation with order number, approved preview, and test containment state.

## Plank Recognition contract
- Never invent physical dimensions from an uncalibrated photograph.
- Current candidate may analyze photo geometry/orientation only.
- Production measurement requires a commissioned scale source: trailer calibration zone/reference marker, device depth/AR, or another validated method.
- Customer always gets a confirm/correct path.

## Lettering Library contract
- Project-owned styles remain isolated to Ike's.
- A reference photo may be staged as visual inspiration; it is not falsely labeled an installable font.
- Future managed intelligence may match/create a usable lettering treatment from the reference after Sea Trial.

## Fleet rule
Photo recognition, reference-to-style, and unified visual design remain Ike-specific candidates until proven through Sea Trial and Fleet Proving Ground evidence. They may later be nominated as reusable Fleet Candidates without copying Ike-specific branding.
