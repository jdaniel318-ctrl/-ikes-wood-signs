Address coverage

The 2026 Q3 Virginia address index includes 685,510 mapped addresses in 14 counties and five cities. Each contractor decides which places they serve.

Counties include Amelia, Charles City, Chesterfield, Dinwiddie, Goochland, Hanover, Henrico, James City, King William, New Kent, Powhatan, Prince George, Surry and York. Cities include Colonial Heights, Hopewell, Petersburg, Richmond and Williamsburg.
