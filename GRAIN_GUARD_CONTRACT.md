# Grain Guard Contract — Dark Sky 8.0.2

## Purpose
Wood-species recognition may assist Ike’s customer flow, but it must never invent confidence or silently convert ambiguity into a price.

## Rules
1. Species pricing uses the same canonical taxonomy in Owner and Customer surfaces: Pine, Cedar, Red Oak, White Oak, Walnut, Hickory.
2. Species evidence is sampled from the detected plank body, not the whole countertop/photo. A single visual observation is evidence, not proof. Ambiguous results request one additional grain photo in positive customer language.
3. Oak-family recognition must not auto-select Red Oak or White Oak from weak visual heuristics. The exact priced species must be resolved by stronger evidence or customer confirmation of the Ike rack selection.
4. Two-photo disagreement is a HOLD on automatic species resolution, not permission to average or guess.
5. Price = active owner species rate per linear foot × customer-confirmed plank length. No species resolution means no customer price.
6. Confidence values are internal decision support. Customer copy stays simple, positive, and action-oriented.
7. Future inventory/rack tags or managed visual models may outrank heuristic evidence when explicitly commissioned, but they must remain project-scoped and auditable.
