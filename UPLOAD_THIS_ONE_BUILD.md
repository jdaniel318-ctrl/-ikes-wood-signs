1. Open Admiral Command, Contracts & Services, select VoyageKeeper Recovery Test and load its vessel draft on the same device.
2. Open a fresh printable owner offer. Tap Open one-page PDF in its header. Previous offer tabs keep the previous build’s print behavior.
3. On the PDF, choose Share, then Print. At US Letter, Portrait and 100%, inspect the single-page preview for the deposit, handoff balance, waived monthly support and final ownership terms. Use Save to Files in the share sheet to keep the PDF.
