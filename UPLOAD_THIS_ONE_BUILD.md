## Set one vessel’s fees

1. Open Admiral Command, then Contracts & Services. Select an existing vessel, or choose Planned new ship and start its draft. To continue an existing offer on this device, choose Load vessel draft.
2. In Admiral fee controls, enter the one-time setup, deposit at agreement, and monthly support. Suggested figures appear below each field. Enter 0 to waive any fee. The deposit is part of setup. The handoff balance updates as you type.
3. Select Save offer fees. After the device confirms the save, open Open printable owner offer. Choose Print / save PDF to produce a portable copy for the owner. The agreement review copy includes the same launch terms in Schedule C.

Under Reuse pricing on this device, choose Save as my rates to remember a set for your Admiral account. On a different vessel, select Use my rates, review the amounts, then Save offer fees. Use suggested fees brings the original suggestions back into the editor. Each saved offer keeps its own prices.

## Rehearse using VoyageKeeper

In the Engine Room, open Business tools, Fleet launch pilot, then VoyageKeeper rehearsal. Open private preview and start a sample request. Rehearse fee setting with VoyageKeeper in Contracts & Services. The public suggested pricing example is for discussion. Individual owner offers come from the saved vessel draft. Invoicing and payment collection follow your existing agreement and billing process.

## Recovery and device records

Ratebook-Source.zip contains complete client source. Ratebook-Rollback.zip restores the previous Berthlight software. Never restore old permissions or business data during software rollback. Browser-local drafts are scoped to the signed-in Admiral and vessel. Keep a private draft export to move an offer between devices. Personal starting rates are saved separately on the current device.
