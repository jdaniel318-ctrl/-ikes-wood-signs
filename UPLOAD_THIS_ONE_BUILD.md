Unzip Folio.zip. Upload the contents of its single folder to the existing Black Flag repository root over Ratebook 8.8.20.163. Replace matching files and keep the others. After deployment, look for 8.8.20.164 · FOLIO in the Engine Room.

## Check the owner offer on iPad

1. In Admiral Command, open Contracts & Services, select VoyageKeeper Recovery Test, and load its saved vessel draft on the same device.
2. Open a fresh printable owner offer. An offer tab opened in Ratebook keeps its original print layout.
3. Choose Print / save PDF. Select US Letter, Portrait and 100%. Check for one page with the deposit, handoff balance, support and final terms visible.
