## What changes

The **Customers** destination and overview shortcut open retained customer
history for the selected vessel. Its records and totals belong to that vessel.
Changing vessels replaces that history. The older singular customer route still
opens Experience, preserving existing links and workflow.

Fleet status distinguishes identity/isolation setup, locally recorded
owner/Captain setup, experience or Sea Trial records, deployment records and
available recovery tools. The first unmet stage drives the highlight and next
instruction. Attention includes a technically ready vessel that still awaits a
Captain order. Local approval does not prove server membership or authority;
available recovery tools do not prove a verified backup or restore.

Opening Project Control from Fleet Compass preserves the current search,
filter and useful return position while its originating Engine session and
route are current. Nested workspace switches keep the original origin.
Signing out or navigating away invalidates an old return callback. Other entry
origins retain their established return destination.

A readiness invocation shares one bounded no-store source read per static
path. A failed read remains failed for that run. The next invocation reads
fresh bytes; an earlier pass cannot hide a later source, JSON, hash or seal
failure. Existing operational and authority queries retain their own behavior.

Ike's branding, own-board photograph flow, one-sign request, complete ZIP
handoff, Preview/Sea Trial contact guards and CNC geometry remain preserved.
This release does not change authentication authority or migrate stored data.

## Verification scope

**FLEETCARE_EVIDENCE.json** records the exact sealed runtime tested and the
SHA-256 of each actual offline suite report. Final packaging checks every named
result, totals, release identity, tested runtime and shipped verifier bytes.
The suite reports are external review evidence, bound by their hashes rather
than inserted into the runtime. They cover targeted workflows, safe navigation,
readiness failures/freshness, actual sealed Engine routes, sealed-loader startup
and tamper holds, and Boardcraft's actual-index photo/wording/ZIP smoke path.

Candidate staging explicitly retains `pass=false` and creates no ZIPs. A final
release requires all six suites to pass against the same runtime. The packager
also checks the exact delivered .62 archive and all 423 baseline filenames,
retained historical bytes, unchanged atomic loader dependencies, an update of
at most 85 files, current America/New_York timestamps, filename-matching ZIP
roots and byte-exact update reconstruction of the full archive.

These are offline checks with synthetic fixtures. They do not claim native
iPad/Safari acceptance, live account/provider acceptance, sent email, inbox
receipt or physical CNC machine acceptance. No deployment is performed.
