WONDERWAKE 15 · LANTERN HARBOR · DANIEL SCHOOL OF EXCELLENCE

1. In the existing Wonderwake, choose each explorer and tap “Keep a progress copy” to back up their work. Save any open painting.
2. Unzip the new package. Open Wonderwake15-LanternHarbor and upload all the top-level files inside that folder together to the root of the existing wonderwake repository. Keep the verification and recovery folders on your device.
3. When GitHub Pages finishes, open https://jdaniel318-ctrl.github.io/wonderwake/ in your usual browser and refresh. Look for “LANTERN HARBOR · FAMILY EDITION 0.11.0” in the footer.

PLAY WITH TWO LEARNERS
Choose “+ Add learner” for each child and use a nickname. Up to 24 local profiles can use the same browser, including three starter explorers. Tap the current explorer’s name at the top to switch. Work in one active game tab. Progress files can be carried to another device using the existing per-learner export and import.

Try counting the spare lanterns in K–2 math as one child and building a bell pattern in music as another. Switch back and use “Continue my adventure.” Use the K–12 curriculum map to explore suggested goals in each grade. The first playable collection contains 24 lessons across the four grade bands.

RECOVERY
Keep a fresh progress copy for each explorer before changing releases. recovery/Wonderwake14-Skybloom.zip holds the preceding application code. The older app predates school checkpoints. To restore a Lantern Harbor progress file and keep its school work, install Lantern Harbor first. Keep your website data on this device.
